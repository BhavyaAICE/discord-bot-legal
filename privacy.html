Privacy Policy

Your data, explained.

This policy describes how Hades MM & Exchange may collect, use, store, and disclose information when you use its Discord bot, verification portal, moderation tools, and Litecoin trade-assistance features.

Effective: October 18, 2026Discord application
Scope matters: The exact information processed depends on which features a Discord server enables and how you use them. Public blockchain data is visible to anyone.

1. Information we may process

  • Discord identifiers and server data: Discord user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel and role IDs, relevant usernames/display names, and configuration needed to operate features.
  • Moderation and security records: warnings, punishments, moderator IDs, verification outcomes, join timestamps, anti-raid or anti-alt risk signals, whitelist entries, and security events.
  • Messages and feature inputs: content you submit to commands, tickets, vouches, deal logs, autoresponders, custom embeds, or other interactive features. The bot may process message content where needed for a feature that is enabled.
  • Trade and escrow information: counterparty identifiers, trade descriptions, agreed USD/LTC amounts, fees, deposit and payout addresses, transaction hashes, payment status, and trade timestamps.
  • Verification portal information: verification responses and technical signals used to prevent abuse. If enabled, hCaptcha or IP/proxy-risk checks may process information such as IP address, browser/device signals, or challenge results.
  • Technical and diagnostic data: error logs, command activity, timestamps, and operational records necessary to maintain and secure the service.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to run commands and requested features; create tickets and trade records; monitor blockchain deposits and payouts; calculate or display trade fees and exchange estimates; provide verification and anti-abuse checks; enforce server rules; maintain reputation and deal logs; troubleshoot errors; protect the bot and users; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Where information may be processed or shared

  • Discord: The bot operates through Discord and uses Discord's API. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own policies.
  • Infrastructure providers: The bot's hosting, database, and logging providers may process data to operate the service.
  • Verification and risk providers: If enabled, hCaptcha and IP/proxy-risk services may receive the technical information required to perform their checks.
  • Blockchain and market-data providers: Blockchain APIs and exchange-rate services may receive requests involving transaction IDs, wallet addresses, or pricing lookups.
  • Public blockchain: Litecoin addresses and transaction details written to the blockchain are public and may remain publicly available indefinitely. They cannot be removed by the bot operator.
  • Discord server members: Information deliberately posted in public channels, public deal logs, vouches, or public/anonymous logging options may be visible according to the selected feature and server permissions. “Anonymous” logging should not be assumed to make an on-chain transaction anonymous.
  • Legal and safety reasons: Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, enforce terms, or protect users and the service.

We do not sell personal information as a business model. Do not use this service if you are not comfortable with the processing described above.

4. Data storage and retention

Feature data may be stored in a database so settings, moderation history, reputation records, tickets, and trade states persist. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain security and accounting records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods can differ by data type and server configuration. Expired tickets or deleted Discord channels do not necessarily mean every related database or log record has been erased.

5. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect stored information and credentials. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Access to bot credentials, database backups, and wallet secrets should be strictly restricted. Never send a seed phrase or private key through Discord or to support staff.
